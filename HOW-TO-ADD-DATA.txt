UECFI AREA 4 — DEPLOYMENT & DATA GUIDE
=======================================
FILES (keep together in ONE folder):
  index.html   the app          sw.js   offline engine
  data.js      YOUR members + prayers (edit this)
  photos/      member photos

ADDING MEMBERS / PRAYERS
  1. Copy photos into the "photos" folder.
  2. Open data.js in Notepad and fill in the members and prayers lists (examples are inside).
  3. Open sw.js and change CACHE_NAME 'uecfi-lyrics-v5' to 'uecfi-lyrics-v6' (next time v7...).
  4. Upload the whole folder. Each device loads the new entries on its next online visit.

DEPLOY: host the folder on any HTTPS static host (Netlify, Cloudflare Pages, GitHub Pages),
then on phones use "Add to Home Screen"/"Install app".

FIRST LOGIN: superadmin / ChangeMe123! (forced change). Change it immediately.

BACKGROUND: the UECFI background art is LOCKED in index.html (section "LOCKED: BRAND BACKGROUND").
It shows in Light and Dark themes. Do not edit that section.
